# Narrow audiences

The 200,000 people a country cover most audiences, down to a town: a local
authority (`county_or_local_authority`) or town (`settlement_name`) holding 1 in
100 adults has about 2,000 of them. When an audience leaves fewer than a few
hundred, `people_pull` draws more from PersonaGen, all in the audience (it needs
a PersonaGen key; with check set, it sizes the audience without drawing). Pass the file it
names as people_file to `people`, `sweep`, `terms` and `probe`, or as "people"
in `suggest`'s suggestions. Figures from them are of that audience, not of adults:
say so in the reply.
