# What it cannot answer

Say so, and do not put something else in its place.

- **Facts the people do not hold:** whether someone has a boiler, when it was
  last serviced, which brand they use. When `people` (action find) finds
  nothing, nothing here counts it, and a match may be another fact ("gas" finds
  people who work in gas supply). Count a stand-in only under its own name
  (homeowners, not boiler owners), and look up the real figure (guide topic
  look_up).
- **How many, from a scan.** Its level is far off, and worst for rare things.
  Give who; pass a published rate as level.
- **Which idea most people would take, or what it will sell.** The order of
  ideas changed with the question; who wanted each did not. Real results, kept
  with `outcome_record`, say which.
- **How big a difference is.** A scan's differences are too small, and scaling
  them up does not fix that.
- **A price, a wording, or which lever matters most.**
- **What an organisation decides.** The people are adults.
- **Hiring, medical, political and other high-stakes decisions about individual
  people.** Keep them out of this. Counting whom a requirement shuts out is not
  such a decision: a job application that asks for a car or an online test, or
  a surgery that books online only, is counted like any other term.
