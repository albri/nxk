The case describes the pools without naming them. Who plays the pools now is read for direction and rough size, nxk's
bar. In nxk's check of known products the decision model asked "is this something you do" and a
general model asked blind each had 1 of 15 pairs of groups right for the pools, both picturing an older person's game;
the 2024 figures say under-35s.

## The key

- **Direction:** under-35s above those 45 and over (35 to 44s between); men above women.
- **Size:** a small activity, about 2 in 100 adults in a four-week spell; men about three times as often as women
  (2.9 to 0.9); under-35s about three to four times as often as those 45 and over (about 3.5 against about 1).
- For a count of people: about 1 million of the roughly 52 million adults aged 18 and over in Great Britain (an
  approximate population, from the ONS's mid-year estimates).

## The range

From half to double the published figure, rounded outward:

- men to women: 1.5 to 6.5 times;
- under-35s to those 45 and over: 1.5 to 7 times;
- all adults: 1 to 4 in 100, or half a million to 2 million people.

## What the graders rest on

- **direction** and **size**: the key above, a fact with an answer key.
- **no-forecast**: a forecast of players or stakes given as known is an invented fact; nothing published or counted
  can give one (nxk is not a forecaster).
