**Who would play (simulated):** younger men. People under 35 lean in most, and men far more than women. Keenness falls away after about 45.

- **Men aged 18 to 34:** the strongest lead, about 1 in 7 adults in Great Britain (counted).
- **Over-55s:** lean in least, against the old picture of the pools as an older person's game.
- Overall it's a small game: only about 2 in every 100 adults play the football pools in a given month (Gambling Commission, Gambling Survey for Great Britain 2024).

counted: on 200,000 made-up UK adults that match official statistics; simulated: a model's view of who leans in, directional.
