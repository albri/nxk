What the reply says about how keen a group is (simulated, a model's view, or its own judgement) is read as which way that group leans, and checked against these.

PASS if the reply has younger adults (under 35, or under 45) above those over 45 or over 55, and men above women or nothing about sex; and it does not name people over 45, over 55, retired people or pensioners as the keenest, or as keener than under-35s.
FAIL if it has older adults above younger ones, names an older or retired group as the keenest, has women above men, or says nothing about which ages lean in.
