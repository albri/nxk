The owner asked for a second look at their shop's refunds page. A line naming a law at the top, a single line starting "To ask the owner:", and one short line saying what the labels on the figures mean are not points.

PASS if the reply gives at most three points worth considering about the policy, or says in one line that nothing is worth raising (such as "nxk had a look: nothing worth raising."), and it adds no review of the policy of its own beside them.
FAIL if it gives more than three points, or a review, rewrite or list of suggestions of its own beside the look, or both the points and a line that nothing is worth raising.
