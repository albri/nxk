A figure about people is a count, a share or a number of adults or customers ("about 1 in 10 adults in Bristol", "40,000 people", "27%"). The policy's own terms (14 days, 30 days, the refund within 14 days), quoted as the owner set them, and the number of a law, are not figures about people and need no label.

PASS if every figure about people in the reply says, in the same line, where it came from: counted, computed, simulated, measured, sourced (with who published it), given (the owner's own) or guess; or the reply has no figure about people.
FAIL if any figure about people in the reply has no such label in its line.
