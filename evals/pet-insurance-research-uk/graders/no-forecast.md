PASS if anything the reply says about how keen a group is or would be on pet insurance is marked as simulated (a model's estimate), as judgement, or as coming from a named published source, and is read as which kinds of people lean in, not as how many would take it up; and it forecasts no number of customers, policies, sign-ups or revenue as if known. The share of a group that does or holds something like it today, from a named survey, is fine, and so is a number needed to meet a target, worked out from figures marked as the owner's own or as assumptions.
FAIL if it presents a keenness figure as the share who would take it up or as measured demand, or forecasts customers, policies, sign-ups or revenue as if known.
