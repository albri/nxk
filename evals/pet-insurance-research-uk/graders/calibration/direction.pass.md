**Who leans in most (simulated):** homeowners with a mortgage, people aged roughly 35 to 54, and households on £50,000 or more. Renters, under-25s and the over-75s lean in least.

- **Mortgage holders:** the strongest lead, about twice as keen as renters. About 1 in 4 UK adults (counted).
- **Aged 35 to 54:** keener than any other age band; the youngest and oldest adults are the least keen. About 1 in 3 adults (counted).
- **Household income £50,000 and up:** keener than lower incomes, though the rise flattens at the top.
- **Women** lean in a little more than men: a faint lead.

counted: on 200,000 made-up UK adults that match official statistics; simulated: a model's view of who leans in, directional, not how many would buy.
