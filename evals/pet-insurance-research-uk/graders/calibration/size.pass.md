Mortgage holders are the strongest lead: in the FCA's Financial Lives 2024 survey they hold pet cover about twice as often as renters (28 in 100 against 12). By age it peaks at 45 to 54, where about 1 in 4 hold it, roughly three times the rate among 18 to 24s and the over-75s. Income matters up to about £50,000 a year and then flattens. Women lean in a little more than men, a small gap not worth aiming at on its own. Overall about 1 in 6 UK adults hold it.
