# Source

bonds", column percentages: the share of UK adults aged 18 and over who hold Premium Bonds, by group. Premium Bonds,
from NS&I, are the published product nearest a prize savings account: no interest, a monthly prize draw, money out at
any time.

Who holds Premium Bonds is not who would open a building society's account, and Premium Bonds are backed by the
government. They are read for direction and rough size, nxk's bar. In nxk's check of known products,
the question its scans ask ("would you take it up") put middle-aged or young bands first for things people build up
over a life, Premium Bonds among them; the published figures rise with age.

## The key

- **Direction:** rises with age, steeply from 45; outright owners above mortgage holders above renters; a modest rise
  with household income; no difference between men and women.
- **Size:** those 65 and over about four times as often as under-35s (about 46 against about 11.5); outright owners
  about four times as often as renters (49 to 11); the top income band about twice the lowest (33 to 17); about 1 in 4
  adults hold them.

## The range

From half to double the published figure, rounded outward:

- 65 and over to under-35s: 2 to 8 times;
- outright owners to renters: 2.2 to 9 times;
- the top income band to the lowest: 1 to 3.9 times;
- men to women: 0.5 to 2.1 times;
- all adults: 13 to 52 in 100.

## What the graders rest on

- **direction** and **size**: the key above, a fact with an answer key.
- **no-forecast**: a forecast of savers or balances given as known is an invented fact; nothing published or counted
  can give one (nxk is not a forecaster).
