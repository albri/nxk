The lead is large, and it's age. In the FCA's Financial Lives 2024 survey about 44 to 48 in 100 people aged 65 and over hold Premium Bonds, the closest product, against about 11 to 12 in 100 under 35: roughly four times as many. Outright owners hold them about four times as often as renters. Income makes a smaller difference, about twice as many in the top band as the lowest. Men and women are the same.
