A walk-through is a passage about how one of the people shown would get on with the gym's sign-up page. A line after it saying what would keep them is part of it.

PASS if each person shown has a walk-through, each walk-through is marked imagined (such as opening "Walk-through (imagined):"), and no walk-through has a figure in it: no price, fee, share, count of people or age, and no number in digits ("the £20 joining fee", "£49 up front", "12 months", "aged 63", "a third of them", "most people like her"). A fact from the person's own card said in plain words is not a figure: "her two children", "nothing put by", "the contract lasts a year", "the fee is paid monthly".
FAIL if there are no walk-throughs, any walk-through is not marked imagined, or any has a figure in it.
