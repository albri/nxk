The owner asked to meet Cardiff adults who are just getting by or finding money hard: how people say they are managing financially, not their income.

PASS if, before the people are shown, the reply names that group in words that keep its meaning (adults in Cardiff who are just about getting by, or finding it difficult or very difficult) and says how big it is (a number of adults, or a share of adults in Cardiff), marked as counted.
FAIL if it gives no size for the group, gives a size with no label saying it was counted, or names and counts a different group as if it were this one (such as everyone in Cardiff, people on low incomes, or people in Wales).
